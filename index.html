Explore the 15 competences in the EntreComp framework for entrepreneurial skills.

Ideas and opportunities

Competences related to spotting, creating and evaluating ideas. This is about seeing opportunities where others don’t, thinking creatively, and taking an ethical and sustainable approach.

Resources

Competences related to knowing yourself and mobilising what you need. Self-awareness, motivation, financial literacy and the ability to engage other people and resources.

Into action

Competences related to turning ideas into practice. Taking the initiative, planning, managing risk, working together and learning along the way – actually doing something with your ideas.
